# STORY NOTES & MYTHOLOGICAL ADAPTATION ANALYSIS

## Ancient Sources
* **Homer's Iliad**: Establishes the divine tension and character profiles of Agamemnon and the Greek commanders.
* **Euripides' *Iphigenia at Aulis***: Source for Iphigenia's dignified acceptance of her sacrifice for the unity and destiny of her people.
* **Aeschylus' *Agamemnon***: Source for the heavy moral weight and tragedy of the sacrifice at Aulis.

## Temporal Match Cut Rationale
The match cut between Iphigenia's hand touching the altar stone at Aulis and Commander Kaelen's hand touching the quantum core of *Aulis-9* demonstrates THREAD's core temporal concept: human moral choices are timeless invariants that echo across millennia.
