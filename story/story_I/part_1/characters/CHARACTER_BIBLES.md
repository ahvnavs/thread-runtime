# STORY I — PART 1: CHARACTER BIBLES & CONTINUITY PROFILES

---

## 1. AGAMEMNON (King of Mycenae & High Commander)

* **Source / Mythology**: Ancient Greek Epic Cycle & Homeric Tradition (Iliad, Euripides' *Iphigenia at Aulis*).
* **Role**: High Commander of the Greek forces; father forced into tragic moral dilemma.
* **Objective**: To lead his people across the sea and fulfill his sacred command without destroying his house.
* **Immediate Want**: For the wind to blow so the army can sail.
* **Deeper Need**: Moral alignment between his duty as king and his love as a father.
* **Fear**: That his indecision will destroy his army, provoke mutiny, and betray his oath.
* **Contradiction**: He commands a thousand ships, yet cannot command a single breath of wind.
* **Beginning State**: Paralyzed by indecision at the flagship prow.
* **Midpoint State**: Broken by grief as Iphigenia approaches the altar.
* **Ending State**: Resigned to destiny as the sudden gale-force winds fill the sails.
* **Visual Identity**: Tall, commanding posture, bronze breastplate, dark navy mantle, weathered face, dark grey-streaked beard.
* **Palette**: Dark slate `#2d2a32`, navy `#19143c`, antique bronze `#d4ac0d`.

---

## 2. IPHIGENIA (Princess of Mycenae)

* **Source / Mythology**: Euripides' *Iphigenia at Aulis*, Aeschylus' *Agamemnon*.
* **Role**: Royal daughter whose voluntary composure and interiority transform tragedy into nobility.
* **Objective**: To step onto the altar willingly, transforming forced sacrifice into a conscious choice for her people.
* **Immediate Want**: To comfort her weeping father and walk toward her fate with unshakeable dignity.
* **Deeper Need**: Agency over her own mortality.
* **Fear**: Fear of the unknown, mastered through quiet courage.
* **Contradiction**: She is the victim of a political dilemma, yet emerges as the most composed and autonomous actor at Aulis.
* **Beginning State**: Walking in quiet reflection toward the cliffside temple.
* **Midpoint State**: Reaching out her hand to touch the ancient sacred granite pillar.
* **Ending State**: Transcending her temporal moment as her choice echoes into the far future.
* **Visual Identity**: White pleated linen robe, dark tousled hair, amber eyes, graceful unhurried stride.
* **Palette**: Pure linen white `#ffffff`, amber `#b7950b`, soft gold `#f1c40f`.

## 3. COMMANDER KAELEN (Far-Future Core Commander)

* **Source / Mythology**: Original THREAD Sci-Fi Invention (Temporal Echo of Iphigenia/Agamemnon).
* **Role**: Chief Operations Officer aboard colony flagship *Aulis-9*.
* **Objective**: To save 50,000 sleeping colonists from total reactor decay.
* **Immediate Want**: To stabilize the collapsing quantum core before cascade failure.
* **Deeper Need**: Fulfilling a lineage of sacrifice that spans millennia.
* **Fear**: Dying in deep space without knowing if the colony fleet survives.
* **Contradiction**: He operates hyper-advanced quantum technology, yet relies on the same primal moral courage as ancient ancestors.
* **Beginning State**: Standing before the pulsing reactor monolith in the dark core chamber.
* **Midpoint State**: Reaching out his cybernetic hand to touch the glowing cyan monolith.
* **Ending State**: Illuminating with bioluminescent energy as the colony starship engines ignite.
* **Visual Identity**: Dark slate tech-coat, bioluminescent cyan iris ring, cybernetic temple seam, calm resolute posture.
* **Palette**: Void black `#020617`, electric cyan `#00e5ff`, emerald green `#5ded2f`.
