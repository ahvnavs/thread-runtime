## 1. STORY & THEMATIC PREMISE

### Premise
On the rainless shores of Aulis, the Greek fleet lies paralyzed by a dead calm. King Agamemnon faces an agonizing choice demanded by fate: sacrifice his daughter Iphigenia to rouse the winds of war, or abandon his command. Centuries across time, aboard a drifting colony vessel in deep space, Commander Kaelen faces a parallel choice: initiate a manual core purge that saves the fleet at the cost of personal annihilation.

### Thematic Premise
Fate is not a force that forces human action; it is a resonance across time where identical moral choices ripple through different worlds.

### Dramatic Question
Will Agamemnon surrender what he loves to fulfill a destiny he cannot escape, and will the unseen echo in the far future yield redemption or endless cycle?

### Emotional Promise
A somber, dignified, and visually striking tragedy where individual human vulnerability is juxtaposed against cold cosmic necessity.

## 2. THE UNSEEN ENTITY & TEMPORAL LAWS

1. **The Unseen Entity**: The supreme governing order above mortals and stars. It possesses no physical form, voice, face, or deity name. Its presence is manifested purely through atmospheric silence, sudden wind surges, glowing runes/symbols, and perfect temporal synchronization.
2. **Quantum Fate Threads**: Past and future are not connected by linear flashbacks, but by **Match-Cut Synchronicity**. When a character in the ancient mythic era touches a focal point (e.g. the altar pillar of Aulis), a character in the far future simultaneously touches a parallel focal point (the quantum core reactor).
